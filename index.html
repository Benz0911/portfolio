TaxZed

Independent product · Accounting SaaS for the Philippines · 2025–Present · in closed beta

A multi-tenant bookkeeping SaaS that automates VAT calculation and accounting entry generation for small Philippine businesses. Most bookkeeping tools aren't built around local tax rules, so TaxZed's accounting and VAT workflows were designed around them from the start.

My role

Full-stack development: Laravel back end, React front end, database design, and deployment on DigitalOcean.

What I built

  • A multi-tenant expense and revenue ledger with automatic VAT from VAT-inclusive totals, calculated on the server rather than trusted from the browser.
  • Accounting entry generation that locks a month's transactions into an accounting period, with controlled administrator unlocking for corrections.
  • A chart of accounts seeded from BIR standard codes and shared across tenants.
  • Supplier management with TIN tracking, and inline supplier creation from the expense workflow.
  • BIR form records for 2550M and 2550Q, tied to accounting periods.
  • A superadmin panel with company impersonation, feedback triage and controlled period unlocking.

Technical challenges

  • Exact VAT arithmetic: the base amount comes from the VAT-inclusive total and the tax is the remainder, so every transaction reconciles without rounding drift.
  • Multi-tenancy in one database: a reusable BelongsToCompany trait applies a global Eloquent scope and fills in company_id automatically.
  • Authentication across origins: Sanctum, CORS and CSRF configured so the separate React front end and Laravel back end work together securely.
  • Keyboard-first entry: shortcuts like Alt+S and Alt+N and arrow-key table navigation for fast, repetitive bookkeeping.

Result

Live at app.taxzed.com in closed beta, with real testers entering bookkeeping data. Expense and revenue entry, VAT computation, accounting generation and BIR form records all work, and development continues ahead of the public launch.

Word Zoo

Independent product · Mobile word game for Android · 2026 · in Google Play closed testing

A word-guessing puzzle game where players rescue animals by spelling words to defeat the villain, Dr. Snuggles. Instead of disconnected puzzles, the levels form a story: beat the villain, rescue animals, and unlock new worlds.

My role

Product vision, game design, level content, art direction, monetization and in-app purchase strategy, Google Play setup, QA and technical direction. I used AI-assisted development for code implementation, architecture exploration and technical problem-solving.

What I built

  • 10 themed worlds with 400+ levels of single-word and multi-word phrase puzzles.
  • Flame-powered gameplay: animated animals, villain reactions, hearts, countdown timers and interactive game states.
  • Banner, skippable interstitial and rewarded ads, with rewarded ads for continues.
  • Cloud save and accounts with Firebase and Google Sign-In, so progress follows players across devices.
  • Quests, daily bonus streaks, leaderboards, coins, hints and hint packs.
  • Google Play in-app purchases for eight planned products across coins, hint packs and ad-free tiers.

Technical challenges

  • Dynamic phrase layout: a two-pass word-packing approach sizes the letter slots to the screen and phrase length, without overlapping the on-screen keyboard.
  • Ad frequency that survives navigation: the "every three levels" counter lives in a persistent AdService singleton instead of screen state.
  • Safe async game callbacks: lifecycle guards on callbacks like onWin and onLose, so delayed calls can't touch disposed widgets.

Result

Feature-complete in about two months and in Google Play closed testing with 12+ testers. Gameplay, progression, cloud save, ads and in-app purchases are in place; the public release is waiting on Google Play's review and launch process.
